CTC Truck Owner – Privacy Policy

Effective: 11 November 2026 · App: CTC Truck Owner (Android package in.ctctransport.truckowner) · Operated for CTC TRANSPORT SERVICE (CT Corporation India Pvt Ltd)

CTC Truck Owner lets truck owners who work with CTC TRANSPORT SERVICE see their trucks, trips, hire statement, payments and vehicle documents, and respond to loads posted by CTC. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

DataWhy
Mobile numberTo send a one-time password (OTP) on WhatsApp and log you in.
Name / company nameTo identify your owner account with CTC.
Trip confirmations, load rate offers, payment requests and amountsTo pass your actions to CTC.
Vehicle documents and photos you upload (RC, insurance, permit, fitness, PUC, and similar papers)To store your vehicle papers and share them with CTC.
Vehicle registration numberTo look up RC, e-Challan and FASTag / toll details for a truck already on your account. The number is sent to our vehicle-data provider (Perione) for that lookup only.

The app also displays records CTC already holds about you (trips, ledger, payments, GST/PAN and bank details). We do not collect your location, contacts, SMS, advertising ID or device identifiers, and the app contains no ads or third-party analytics.

How data is used and shared

  • Data is used only to provide the app's features.
  • Your account, trips, payments and documents are shared with CTC TRANSPORT SERVICE, the company that registered your mobile number.
  • Your mobile number is shared with our WhatsApp messaging provider (AiSensy) so the login OTP can be delivered.
  • A vehicle registration number is shared with Perione only when you open RC, e-Challan or toll details for that truck.
  • We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.
  • We may disclose data if required by Indian law.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS to https://ctc-supp.tmsprime.in. Your login token is stored in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore).

Retention and deletion

You can ask us to delete your CTC Truck Owner login and documents you uploaded by emailing support@tmsprime.in from, or quoting, your registered mobile number. We act within 30 days. Trip, ledger and payment records may be retained by CTC as required for accounting and tax law.

Children

The app is intended for business users aged 18 and over.
